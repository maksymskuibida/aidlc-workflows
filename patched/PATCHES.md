# The series

Twenty-nine patches on upstream main `1bdd687e9` (2026-10-29), one commit each on `main`, in
five groups: upstream's own fixes we carry (A), our fixes that stand on the base alone (B),
our fixes that need something before them (C), our improvements (D), and our fixes that
build on an improvement, placed after it (E). No fix in A–C depends on an improvement.
"Upstream candidate" means a single-cause fix with its own test that can go upstream as its
own pull request, after an issue. The numbers are the ones the patches had on `v2.11.1`, so
references stay stable; a patch upstream merged keeps its number in Merged upstream below.

The base is a commit of upstream's `main`, not a release tag: upstream merged our fixes
there first. It holds everything in stable `v2.11.2` (2026-10-28), which is
`v2.11.2-preview.20261027.2` plus its promote commit; `main` is that preview plus 50 commits,
among them the record of `v2.11.2` (#3226). Of those 50, the one team-Unit change besides ours,
[#3283](https://github.com/awslabs/aidlc-workflows/pull/3283) (a teammate who adopts a published
Unit keeps the owner's answered questions), changes only `adoptUnit`, which no patch touches.
Upstream check: 2026-10-29, upstream main `1bdd687e9`. The Needs column says what a patch needs in this series; a note says what it
needs to go upstream.

When the series moved from `v2.11.1` to upstream main, every dropped patch's tests ran on
the new base without the patch, in upstream's version and in the fork's: all pass, except
the case Merged upstream names for 11. Every remaining patch's own tests pass on the new base
with the series.
